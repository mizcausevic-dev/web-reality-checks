### script.json for launch films
Same shape as episodes plus `"tag": "Launch film"` (or `"Quiz"`) and `"kicker": "Official trailer"` / `"Quiz"` etc.; `n`: 101–103 for commercials, 111–113 for quizzes; `slug`; `title` ≤5 words; `fullTitle`. Commercial narration 140–185 words, 6–8 scenes, scene ids like `hook`, `problem`, `method`, `proof`, `range`, `cta`. Persistent label: per-scene `sim`: `"PROMOTIONAL FILM"` default; use `"FOOTAGE FROM EPISODE NN"` when showing real frames and `"CONCEPTUAL ILLUSTRATION"` for diagrams that are not product output. Embed real frames with Remotion `Img` + `staticFile('launch/<name>.png')`: copy the PNGs you use into `video/public/launch/` (only from rendered episodes 01–20 `frames/` and `thumbnail.png`; nothing else is guaranteed to exist). The commercial must show real product (thumbnails/frames and real evidence numbers) wherever possible and end on the CTA: the repository URL on screen and spoken as "the repository, linked below".

### Quiz script.json
Scenes in order: `hook`, then for each question `qN` (narrates the question and the three options, then **`"think": 6`** seconds of silent countdown are inserted by the tool) and `aN` (narrates the answer and explanation), then `close`. The ask scene's on-screen duration = speech + 6 s; render the countdown (ring or bar) only during the last 6 s using `dur` (`t >= dur - 6`). Show the question and A/B/C options fully from the scene's start; on the reveal scene highlight the correct option in green, dim the others, show the explanation, and a chip "Episode NN" with the evidence value. Quiz narration ≈ 330–400 words total. `captions` per scene ≤ 90 chars/line. Quizzes are built with `tools/build-quiz.mjs` from a single narration take (`raw.mp3`), so write narration as natural paragraphs, one paragraph per scene, in scene order.
